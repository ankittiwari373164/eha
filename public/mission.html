Mission & vision

Making homeownership possible for everyday Australians.

Why EHA exists, what we’re working towards, and the values that guide how we work.

Our mission

Breaking down the barriers to a first home.

Our mission is to make first homeownership more accessible and affordable by helping break down the financial barriers that prevent hardworking Australians from owning a place to call their own.

Through smarter, more economical home designs, affordable house-and-land packages and potential low-deposit purchase pathways, we strive to provide practical solutions that help eligible first home buyers take their first step towards ownership.

Our vision

An Australia where homeownership is within more people’s reach.

We envision a future where more Australians have a genuine opportunity to own a safe, comfortable and affordable home. We aim to be recognised for transparent guidance, thoughtful housing solutions and a commitment to helping first home buyers make informed decisions.

Our purpose

To bridge the gap between the dream of homeownership and the financial realities faced by first home buyers, one smarter housing choice at a time.

Transparent

How we decide

The questions we ask ourselves

When we're not sure what to recommend, we come back to these.

  1. 01
    Is it affordable long-term?Not just today's repayment, but in five years.
  2. 02
    Would we explain it the same way to family?If not, we reword it.
  3. 03
    Is the buyer making the decision?Our job is to inform, not to push.
  4. 04
    Did we say what we don't know?Uncertainty gets mentioned, not hidden.
Why it matters

The problem we're trying to help with

For many Australians, the gap between rent and a mortgage isn't the hard part. Saving the deposit while paying rent is. Every year spent saving is a year of prices moving.

We think part of the answer is smarter homes, not just smaller ones. Sensible designs, well-chosen land, and knowing which help is out there can bring the first step a lot closer.
