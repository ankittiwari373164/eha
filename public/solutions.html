A rough guide to where you might start

Most people end up combining two of these. That's normal.

"I've got a deposit but prices feel out of reach"

Start with smarter economical designs. A well-planned home can cut costs without feeling smaller.

"I want a new home in a growing area"

House-and-land packages let you see the whole cost upfront: land, build and siteworks.

"I'm still saving and it feels slow"

Look at low-deposit pathways first. Some schemes can shorten the saving time a lot.

House & land, explained

What usually sits inside a package price

Every builder and estate is different, so always read the inclusions list. These are the things we check with you before you get attached to a number.

  1. 01
    The landLot size, frontage, and whether the title has registered yet.
  2. 02
    The base buildThe home design with its standard inclusions.
  3. 03
    Site costsSoil, slope, retaining and connections can move the price.
  4. 04
    Developer requirementsFencing, landscaping or facade rules some estates insist on.
  5. 05
    Driveway & extrasOften not included. Worth asking early.
A practical tip: get your finance conversation started before picking a package. Knowing your realistic borrowing range saves a lot of disappointment later. We can point you to licensed brokers if you don't have one.
