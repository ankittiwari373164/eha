How it works

A simple journey from uncertainty to a clearer next step.

Here’s exactly what happens from the moment you start the free assessment.

1

Tell us about your goals

Complete the free initial assessment in five short steps: where you’d like to buy, your approximate budget, deposit savings, timeframe and contact details.

⏱ About 3 minutes
2

We review your options

Our team considers the information you share alongside potential housing pathways, designs, house-and-land packages and possible low-deposit options.

⏱ Within 1 business day
3

Personal follow-up call

A real person calls to talk through what we found, answer your questions and learn more about what matters to you.

⏱ 15–20 minute chat
4

Explore homes & locations

We share relevant designs and packages that suit your budget and preferred areas, with clear information on what’s included.

⏱ At your pace
5

Connect with the right professionals

Where needed, we point you to licensed brokers, lenders and conveyancers for formal eligibility checks and advice.

⏱ When you’re ready
6

Move towards ownership

With clearer information and a realistic plan, you decide the next step. No pressure, no obligation.

⏱ Your decision
What we ask, and don’t

Simple questions. No sensitive documents.

  • Preferred state and approximate budget range
  • Roughly how much you’ve saved
  • When you’re hoping to buy
  • Name, email and mobile so we can follow up

We will never ask for payslips, bank statements, tax file numbers or identity documents through the website.

Not a credit check

Your credit file is never touched.

Not pre-approval

We help you explore, lenders decide.

No obligation

Stop any time, for any reason.

Fast

Usually a call within a day.

Good to know

Process questions

How long does the assessment take?
About three minutes. Five short steps, location, budget, deposit, timeframe and your contact details.
Will you check my credit score?
No. The initial assessment does not involve any credit check. Never send us bank statements, payslips or identity documents through the website.
Does this mean I’m approved for a home loan?
No. EHA’s initial review is not credit advice, formal loan pre-approval or a guarantee of finance. Lending decisions are made by lenders based on their own criteria.
What happens to my information?
We use it to respond to your enquiry and discuss options. It’s stored securely and only accessible to authorised EHA staff. See our Privacy page for details.
