Common questions

Questions first home buyers ask

Do I need a large deposit to enquire?
No. The assessment is designed for buyers at every stage of saving. Any finance or deposit pathway depends on your circumstances and the relevant lender’s criteria, we’ll help you understand what might be realistic.
Is the initial assessment really free?
Yes. The initial homeownership options assessment is free and there is no obligation to proceed with anything.
Does this mean I’m approved for a home loan?
No. EHA’s initial review is not credit advice, formal loan pre-approval or a guarantee of finance. Lending decisions are made by lenders based on their own criteria.
What if I’m still researching?
That’s completely fine. Many people contact us early. We’ll help you understand potential next steps and only send marketing if you opt in.
Are “economical homes” tiny homes?
No. Economical means well-considered layouts and practical design choices that prioritise comfortable living and responsible budgets, full-size family homes, not tiny homes.

Before you start

The costs people forget to budget for

The deposit gets all the attention, but it's rarely the only upfront cost. Here's a rough list to keep in mind. Your actual costs depend on your state, the property and any schemes you're eligible for.

We'll go through these with you on the first call.

  1. 01
    Stamp duty / transfer dutyMany states offer first home buyer exemptions or concessions, depending on price.
  2. 02
    Lenders Mortgage InsuranceUsually applies under a 20% deposit, unless a scheme covers it.
  3. 03
    Conveyancing & legalA conveyancer or solicitor handles the contract and settlement.
  4. 04
    Building & pest / inspectionsWorth doing on established homes; staged inspections for new builds.
  5. 05
    Loan & government feesApplication, valuation, registration and mortgage fees vary.
  6. 06
    Moving & setting upConnections, insurance, window coverings, the small stuff that adds up.

A quick note on how we work

We're not a bank and we don't approve loans. What we do is help you understand the housing options that might suit your budget, and connect you with the right licensed people when you're ready.

If after a chat it turns out now isn't the right time, we'll tell you, and suggest what to work on in the meantime. That's a perfectly good outcome too.

Three things we'll never do
  • Tell you you're "approved" when you're not
  • Ask for bank statements or ID through the website
  • Pass your details around without your consent
