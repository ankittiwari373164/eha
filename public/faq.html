FAQ

Questions first home buyers ask us.

Can’t find your answer? Request a callback and we’ll talk it through.

Do I need a large deposit to enquire?
No. The assessment is designed for buyers at every stage of saving. Any finance or deposit pathway depends on your circumstances and the relevant lender’s criteria, we’ll help you understand what might be realistic.
Is the initial assessment really free?
Yes. The initial homeownership options assessment is free and there is no obligation to proceed with anything.
What if I’m still researching?
That’s completely fine. Many people contact us early. We’ll help you understand potential next steps and only send marketing if you opt in.
How long does the assessment take?
About three minutes. Five short steps, location, budget, deposit, timeframe and your contact details.
Does this mean I’m approved for a home loan?
No. EHA’s initial review is not credit advice, formal loan pre-approval or a guarantee of finance. Lending decisions are made by lenders based on their own criteria.
What are low-deposit pathways?
Depending on eligibility, some buyers may be able to purchase with less than a 20% deposit, for example through government schemes or lender products. We help you explore what may be available to you and point you to licensed professionals.
What is LMI?
Lenders Mortgage Insurance may apply when a deposit is under 20% of the property value. It protects the lender, not you, and its cost varies. Some government schemes can help eligible buyers avoid it.
Will you check my credit score?
No. The initial assessment does not involve any credit check. Never send us bank statements, payslips or identity documents through the website.
Are the homes on your site real listings?
No. Images on this website are illustrative concepts that show the style of practical, economical homes we discuss. Actual availability, designs and pricing are confirmed individually.
Are “economical homes” tiny homes?
No. Economical means well-considered layouts and practical design choices that prioritise comfortable living and responsible budgets, full-size family homes, not tiny homes.
Which states do you cover?
We currently discuss options across Victoria, New South Wales, Queensland, South Australia and Western Australia. Choose “Other / not sure” in the assessment if you’re elsewhere.
Can I choose my own land?
Often, yes. We can discuss house-and-land packages as well as options where you already have, or are looking for, a block.
What happens to my information?
We use it to respond to your enquiry and discuss options. It’s stored securely and only accessible to authorised EHA staff. See our Privacy page for details.
Can I ask you to delete my details?
Yes. Email us and we’ll handle your request in line with applicable privacy law.

Glossary

Words you'll hear a lot

Short, plain-English definitions.

Deposit
The part of the price you pay upfront. The rest is borrowed.
LMI
Lenders Mortgage Insurance. Protects the lender when your deposit is under 20%.
Pre-approval
A lender's conditional indication of how much they may lend you.
Titled land
Land that's officially registered and ready to build on.
Settlement
The day ownership transfers and funds are paid.
Stamp duty
A state tax on property purchases. First home buyers often get concessions.
Inclusions
What's included in a build price, from flooring to fixtures.
Conveyancer
The professional who handles the legal side of buying.
