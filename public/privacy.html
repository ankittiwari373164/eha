Privacy

How we handle the information you share.

Draft for review before launch, must be reviewed against the Australian Privacy Principles.

On this pageWhat we collectHow we use itStorage & CRMMarketingYour rightsContact

What we collect

When you submit the assessment, contact or callback forms, EHA collects your name, contact details, preferred location, approximate budget, deposit, purchase timeframe and any optional information you provide. We also record basic technical details such as the page you submitted from and marketing campaign tags.

We do not request bank statements, identity documents, tax file numbers or other highly sensitive information through this website.

How we use it

We use these details to respond to your enquiry, discuss potential housing options, and arrange follow-up you have requested.

Storage & CRM

Enquiries are stored in EHA’s secure administration system, accessible only to authorised staff with individual logins. Enquiries may also be recorded in our customer relationship management system (Zoho CRM) so our team can manage follow-up. Zoho may store data outside Australia depending on the data centre configured.

Marketing

Optional marketing communications require a separate opt-in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Contact

Email privacy@eha.com.au.

Before publication, EHA must add its legal entity details, contact details, data retention practices, service providers, overseas disclosure details and a reviewed Australian Privacy Principles policy.
